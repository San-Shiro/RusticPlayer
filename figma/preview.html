🚀 How to Import into Figma in 2 Seconds

Step 1 • Click Button
Click "Copy SVG to Clipboard" above (or run .\figma\copy_to_clipboard.ps1 in terminal).
Step 2 • Open Figma
Switch to your installed Figma app, create or open a canvas.
Step 3 • Paste (Ctrl+V)
Press Ctrl+V. Figma immediately instantiates native vector frames, layers, gradients, and text!
